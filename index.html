How this works Welcome to your Content Deck

Never stare at a blank screen again. Every card is a complete plan for one lifestyle post, so you can go from "what do I post?" to filming in minutes.

  1. Pick a bucket below, or tap Deal me a card for a surprise.
  2. Tap the card to flip it. The back gives you the hook and prompt, a What to film shot list, a caption starter, and why brands love that kind of video.
  3. Fill in the highlighted blanks with your own life. The words inside are just examples.
  4. Plan a Batch Day. Add up to 5 cards and get every shot in one list, so you can film a week of content in one go.
  5. Edit with the timer, then tap Posted it. That card leaves the shuffle so you never repeat yourself, but it stays in its bucket to look back on.
The buckets

Tip: Rotate through a different bucket each day so your feed stays balanced. GRWM and Day in My Life build connection; Faves & Finds is where your affiliate links shine.
