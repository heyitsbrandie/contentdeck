The Real Life Content Deck

Aesthetic meets real life. Thirty videos with the words, the shots and the timer, for creators with too many interests for one niche.

How this works
  1. Deal a card or pick one from the list.
  2. Type your details into the blanks, then copy your hook and your caption.
  3. Add cards to your batch day. Your shots combine into one list, grouped so you can film them together.
  4. Time your edit and try to beat 30 minutes.
