How this works Welcome to your Content Deck

Never stare at a blank screen again. Every card is a complete brief for one post, so you can go from "what do I post?" to filming in under five minutes.

  1. Pick a bucket below, or tap Deal me a card for a surprise.
  2. Tap the card to flip it. The back gives you four things: the hook and script, B-roll and pacing cues, a caption starter, and the brand angle (why this video attracts brand deals).
  3. Copy it. Use Copy full card for your notes app, or Copy caption to paste straight into your post.
  4. Start the focus timer and film while the idea is fresh.
  5. Tap "I posted today" to build your streak. Tap the heart on any card to save it for later.
The buckets
  • Authority Building shows what you know.
  • Inbound Bait gets brands to notice you.
  • Story builds connection.
  • Trend helps you reach new people.
  • Behind the Scenes builds trust.
  • Offer turns followers into buyers.
  • Yap Ideas are easy talking-head prompts for low-energy days.

Tip: Rotate through a different bucket each day so your feed stays balanced, and fill in the ___ blanks with your own niche.
